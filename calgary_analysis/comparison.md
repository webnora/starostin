# Comparative Analysis: OE *dōn* vs OHG *tuōn*
## PIE *dhe- в западногерманских языках

---

## One-page Summary

### Research Question

**PIE *dhe-* «ставить, помещать» → PGmc *dōną* «делать»**

> Проявляется ли широкозначность уже в древнейший период?
> Различаются ли стратегии семантического развития в OE и OHG?

## Sacred Specialization: Different Strategies

### Old English: Performatives in Prayer

**Формула:** *dō + pronoun + adjective*

Примеры:
- *dō me halne* «сделай меня здоровым/целым»
- *Dryhten, dō mē þīne mildheortnesse* «Господи, сделай мне Твою милость»
- *dō mē cwicne* «сделай меня живым»

**Тип:** Direct address, performative utterances

---

### Old High German: Collocations with Nouns

**Формула:** *noun + tuōn/tān*

Примеры:
- *riuwa tuōn* «творить покаяние» (букв. «делать покаяние»)
- *riuwa tān* (конъюнктив) «творить покаяние»

**Тип:** Lexical collocation, noun compound

### Hypotheses

**Почему OE шире, чем OHG?**

1. **Genre diversity:** OE corpus включает poetry, prose, legal texts
2. **Translation strategies:** Different approaches to religious texts
3. **Celtic substratum:** Possible influence on semantic expansion
4. **Early grammaticalization:** OE *dōn* begins auxiliarization earlier

## Key Findings for Conference

### ✓ Finding 1: Archaic Broad Meaning Confirmed

**76,6% noise** in OE (VII–XI) + **48,4%** in OHG (IX) = **Broad meaning is archaic!**

**Implication:** PIE *dhe-* likely had broad semantics already in the proto-period

---

### ✓ Finding 2: Different Sacred Strategies

| Language | Strategy | Example |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **OE** | Performatives | *dō me halne* |
| **OHG** | Collocations | *riuwa tuōn* |
| **Latin** | Performatives | *facere* |
| **Greek** | Compounds | *poieō* |

**Implication:** Sacred specialization is IE tendency, but strategies vary

---

### ✓ Finding 3: Early Grammaticalization in OE

**Evidence:**
- Higher noise percentage (76,6%)
- Periphrastic constructions
- Future and modal uses

**Implication:** OE *dōn* → NHG *tun* (aux) shows earlier trajectory

---

## Conclusion

### Main Contributions

1. ✓ **Confirms archaic nature** of broad semantics for *dhe-* → *dōną*
2. ✓ **Shows different strategies** in sacred domain (performatives vs collocations)
3. ✓ **Demonstrates earlier grammaticalization** in OE than OHG
4. ✓ **Provides empirical evidence** for PIE reconstruction

### Typological Significance

**Implications for IE studies:**
- Broad semantics of *dhe-* is reconstructible for PIE
- Sacred specialization is IE areal feature
- Grammaticalization pathways are language-specific
